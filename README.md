# omarchy calendar

Welcome to the official homepage for **omarchy calendar** — your ultimate scheduling and time-management companion. 

## About omarchy calendar
**omarchy calendar** is designed to help you organize your life seamlessly. Whether you are managing personal appointments, coordinating professional meetings, or tracking daily tasks, our application provides a unified, intuitive interface to keep you on top of your schedule.

## Core Functionality
- **Unified Scheduling:** View and manage all your events, tasks, and reminders in one clean dashboard.
- **Cross-Platform Sync:** Access your schedule on the go. Your data syncs in real-time across your desktop and mobile devices.
- **Smart Reminders:** Get timely push notifications and email reminders so you never miss a meeting.
- **Conflict Prevention:** Automatically detect schedule overlaps and prevent double-booking.

## Google Calendar Integration & Data Usage Transparency
To provide you with a seamless scheduling experience, **omarchy calendar** integrates with Google Calendar. 

We are committed to complete transparency regarding how we use your data. When you choose to connect your Google account, our app requests specific permissions (OAuth scopes) to access your Google Calendar data. 

**Why do we need this access?**
- **To display your events:** We need read access to show your existing Google Calendar events within the omarchy calendar interface.
- **To create and edit events:** We need write access so that when you create or update an event in omarchy calendar, it automatically updates your Google Calendar.
- **To prevent double-booking:** We check your availability to ensure new meetings do not overlap with your existing Google Calendar commitments.

**What we DO NOT do:**
- We **do not** use your Google Calendar data for advertising, marketing, or retargeting purposes.
- We **do not** sell your data to third parties.
- We **do not** allow human access to your calendar data (except for verified security, legal, or explicit support reasons as outlined in our Privacy Policy).

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere strictly to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
