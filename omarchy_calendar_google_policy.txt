Privacy Policy Addendum for omarchy calendar (Google Calendar API Integration)

To comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, omarchy calendar adheres to the following practices regarding your Google data. This text can be copied directly into your repository (e.g., PRIVACY.md or within your main privacy policy).

1. Google Calendar API Scopes Requested
omarchy calendar may request access to one or more of the following Google Calendar API scopes depending on the features you use:
- https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar (Full access to read, create, edit, and delete events and calendars)
- https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly (Read-only access to view your calendars and events)
- https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (Access to read and write events on your calendars)

2. Limited Use Disclosure (Required by Google)
omarchy calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Exact Data Accessed
When you connect your Google Calendar, omarchy calendar accesses specific data to function properly, including event titles, start and end times, attendee lists, event descriptions, and location data. We do not access, process, or store your Google account password.

4. Strict Purpose Limitation
Your Google Calendar data is used strictly to provide and improve the core, user-facing features of omarchy calendar. This includes displaying your daily schedule, syncing new events across your devices, preventing double-bookings, and sending you timely meeting reminders. We do not use your calendar data for any other purposes.

5. No Human Access
No human at omarchy calendar will read your Google Calendar data, except under the following strict, limited conditions:
- You provide explicit, affirmative consent for us to view a specific event to resolve a customer support request.
- It is absolutely necessary for security purposes (e.g., investigating abuse, fraud, or fixing a critical system bug).
- It is required to comply with applicable local, state, or federal laws.

6. Data Transfer & Sharing Restrictions
omarchy calendar will NEVER sell your Google Calendar data, nor will we use it for advertising, retargeting, or marketing purposes. We will only transfer this data to third parties if necessary to provide the core service (e.g., secure cloud hosting providers), for security purposes, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger/acquisition.

7. Data Retention & Revocation of Access
You can revoke omarchy calendar's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). If you choose to disconnect your account or delete your omarchy calendar profile, all associated Google Calendar data stored on our servers will be permanently and irreversibly deleted.
